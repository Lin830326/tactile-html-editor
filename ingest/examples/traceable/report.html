Quarter in brief

During the third quarter, Brightpine Holdings kept its momentum across all four operating segments. Total consolidated revenue reached $482,000,000, up 14.2% from Q3 2025, led by Enterprise Solutions at $214.6M.

Gross profit rose to $197.6M, a gross margin of 41.0%. Operating income was $128.4M and the operating margin was 26.6%, compared with 28.0% a year earlier, as the company continued to invest in product development and regional sales teams.
